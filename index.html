Ashlee Mirizio

PhD Student in Informatics @ Indiana University, Bloomington

Focus: Computing, Culture, and Society

I am currently an Informatics PhD student at Indiana University’s Luddy School of Informatics, Computing, and Engineering in the Computing, Culture, and Society (CCS) track. My background is in business and technology, having worked previously as a management consultant and in a technology company.

Currently, I am studying the ideologies and logics that underpin various Silicon Valley and AI sub-cultures, with a focus on the ways in which these ideologies and logics move into culture. I have also published on datafied and computational ways of knowing in sensitive health domains and the ways in which the metaphor of “monoculture” has migrated into computing discourse. I use a mixed-methods approach, drawing on the fields of science and technology studies, critical AI studies, cultural studies, and history.

I am advised by Nathan Ensmenger, Elizabeth Kaziunas, Rachel Plotnick.
